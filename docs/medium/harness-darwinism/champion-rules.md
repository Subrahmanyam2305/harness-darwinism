# Generation 7 Champion Rules

- Source genome: `demo-evolution-3_g7_05`
- Origin genome: `demo-evolution-3_g5_02`
- Score: `5.60616859275494`
- Training seed: `105`
- Episode budget: `200` steps
History budget: `25` turns

1. Collect wood before anything else. Place a crafting table early and craft a wood pickaxe as soon as you have enough wood, then craft a wood sword immediately—never explore without a weapon.
2. Progress through tool tiers without delay: wood pickaxe → mine stone (at least 4) → craft stone pickaxe and stone sword within 5 steps of getting the wood pickaxe. Then immediately seek coal and iron ore by looking for cave entrances on hillsides or digging downward at least 3 blocks.
3. To find stone QUICKLY: face downward and dig with your wood pickaxe. Stone is always 2-4 blocks below the surface. Do NOT wander looking for surface stone—dig straight down. If you don't see stone after mining 2 dirt blocks, move 5 steps and dig again. You MUST have stone within 8 steps of crafting the wood pickaxe.
4. Once you have iron ore and coal, place a furnace immediately, smelt iron ingots, and craft an iron pickaxe and iron sword. Each tier transition (stone→iron) should take no more than 15 steps.
5. Eat plants or kill cows for food PROACTIVELY when health drops below 7/9 or food drops below 5/9. Drink water when thirst drops below 5/9—always top off near a lake. Sleep when energy drops below 3/9 or at nightfall, positioning yourself in a safe spot first.
6. Do NOT repeat actions already completed; always check inventory before crafting or collecting. Never re-collect materials you already have enough of. If stuck for more than 3 steps, read your notes, check inventory, and pursue the next uncompleted achievement.
7. At night, prioritize safety: stay near shelter, keep weapon equipped, and avoid unnecessary exploration. If you must move at night, always have a sword equipped.
8. After each major crafting milestone (table placed, wood tools, stone tools, iron tools), immediately write a note listing: current inventory, achievements unlocked, and your specific next 3 actions with exact steps (e.g., "dig down 3 blocks to find stone"). Read notes every 10 steps to avoid losing track.
9. ANTI-STALL RULE: If you have been alive for 30+ steps and do not yet have stone tools, IMMEDIATELY dig downward to find stone and craft stone pickaxe + stone sword. If 50+ steps and no iron tools, dig down aggressively to find coal and iron. Never spend more than 5 consecutive steps on food/water/sleep management.
